**06** Questions

**Who is Checkmate Dynamics?**

A robotics hardware company in Dallas, Texas. We design motion, sensing and sync hardware (SmartStepper, SmartServo G, Contour, the Sync Beacon and Hub 8 Pro) and build station products from it.

**What happens when something breaks?**

Every Leader joint is the same kind of node, so a repair is a swap, not a visit. Planned service plans include advance replacement; terms are confirmed before a plan starts.

**Do you see our data?**

No. Recordings stay on your machines and everything works on-prem. We see only the reports you choose to send us.

**Do the devices touch our network?**

No. The beacon and Contour are USB devices with no radio and no network connection. The recorder plugin runs on your PC.

**Will the beacon affect our training data?**

It should not: the beacon is a separate USB device and changes nothing on your cameras. We walk through the details on a call.

**We already sync our cameras in hardware.**

Keep it. Hardware sync ties those cameras together; the beacon checks every stream on the station, including webcams, joint logs and wrist depth, and certifies each episode.

**Can we try it first?**

Start with a Skew Audit on one station. Register your interest and we agree the details on a call.
