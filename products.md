# One family of nodes.

Source: https://checkmatedynamics.com/products.html

Products | Four families · one bus

Every product is a finished unit that works on its own and joins the same USB-C bus as the rest. Learn one and you know the next: same cable, same status light, same app.

**A** Move

## Motor nodes with everything inside.

Driver, encoder, controller and gearbox built in and calibrated as one unit. Reducers are part of the nodes, not separate parts.

**A1** SmartStepper 17 From $79

Closed-loop NEMA 17. Board, direct-drive node, or with a 25:1 metal cycloidal.

**A2** SmartServo G From $89

Brushless servo for 2804 and 5208 gimbal motors, direct drive or cycloidal. The Leader’s joints.

**B** Sense

## The scene around the robot, the angle inside the joint.

Contour | **54 × 42 zones**

Wireframe from CAD

**B1–B2** Contour, Contour Wrist Waitlist

Metric time-of-flight depth over USB-C, synced to your cameras. The Wrist version rides on the gripper.

**B3** Output encoder $29

Reads a reducer’s output angle directly. Standard on geared nodes, sold for retrofit.

**C** Sync

## An instrument for the station.

The Sync Beacon doesn’t measure the scene or a joint. It is a clock your cameras film, so every stream can be placed on one timeline and checked against the others. That’s why it has a family of its own.

[Sync Beacon](https://checkmatedynamics.com/sync-beacon.html) | [Sync Kit](https://checkmatedynamics.com/sync-kit.html)

A 14-day timing check is $1,250 per station; owning a beacon is $999. Patent pending.
