What this site does

It shows pages, images and type that we host ourselves. Fonts are served from this site. It sets no cookies and uses no local storage for tracking. There are no analytics, advertising or social-media scripts.

What the host sees

Any web host keeps ordinary server logs, such as your IP address, the page and the time. We do not use them to track people or build profiles.

Links to other sites

Some figures link to their sources on other sites. Those sites have their own privacy practices.
