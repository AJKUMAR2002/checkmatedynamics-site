Start Checkmate Link (bridge) to see your devices

The bridge runs on this PC and talks to the board on your network. In a terminal:

cd %USERPROFILE%\checkmatedynamics
py -I software\app\beta\run.py

This page finds it on 127.0.0.1 port 8790 and keeps looking. Use Chrome or Edge, and allow access to devices on this computer if the browser asks.
